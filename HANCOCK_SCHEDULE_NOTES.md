# Hancock schedule data, 2026-27

Open `/?data=hancock.json` to use this calendar. The file covers every date from August 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027, with no overlapping date assignments.

## Sources and transcription

- User-provided `SY27bellschedule8.5x11.pdf`, one page, with the A, B, and B-Friday bells and the calendar marked **REV 09-01-2026**.
- School calendar page: https://www.hancockhs.org/apps/pages/index.jsp?uREC_ID=1053640&type=d&pREC_ID=2232468
- Dates were transcribed from the calendar cells, rather than generated by alternating weekdays.
- Gray A and maroon B follow the source calendar. The display uses dark gray `#666666`, maroon `#99002B`, and darker maroon `#730020` for B-Fri; these are display choices, not certified brand color codes.

## Bell schedules

All three schedules start with passing at 8:00 a.m. and the first class at 8:05 a.m. A includes periods 1-4; B and B-Fri include periods 5-7 and Academic Lab. Passing periods and check-out are included explicitly. A and B end at 3:15 p.m.; B-Fri ends at 2:16 p.m.

Period 3 or 7 spans three lunch windows: A Lunch 11:21 a.m.-12:06 p.m., B Lunch 12:06-12:51 p.m., and C Lunch 12:51-1:36 p.m. Combined period/lunch labels indicate the applicable window, not that every student has all three lunches.

## Exceptions and assumptions

- **June 4, 2027:** the calendar marks B-Friday, although the separate B FRIDAYS list omits it. Included as B-Fri, producing 15 shortened dates.
- **Testing:** October 14, 2026, April 13, 2027, and April 15, 2027 have testing marks. The source explicitly calls the April dates best guesses pending confirmation. These dates display testing notices without invented bell times.
- **Special events:** December 18 is Winter Wonder Fest, May 28 is Mental Health Day, and June 11 is Field Day. The PDF provides no event bell times, so these display all-day notices asking users to check school announcements. June 11 also has a quarter-end marker.
- **Quarter-end markers:** October 23, January 22, and April 2 retain their gray A schedule. No separate C-schedule dates can be identified in the grid, and no C bell schedule is supplied.
- **Report card pickup:** November 2 and April 12 display a no-regular-classes notice; no pickup times are supplied.
- **Professional development:** green cells display no school for students, including November 11 and February 23 exactly as shown in this Hancock calendar.
- Black weekday cells are no-school dates; Saturdays and Sundays are weekends. Blank weekdays before the August 17 staff-development week and after June 14 are treated as summer break. June 14 itself is a staff-development day.
- The printed June grid includes an impossible June 31. It is omitted.

## Coverage

There are 85 A dates, 70 B dates, and 15 B-Fri dates. Six additional weekdays have testing or special-event notices, for 176 student calendar dates in total. The remaining dates cover professional development, report card pickup, holidays/breaks, weekends, and summer break.
